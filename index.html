Gary Finds Out uploader

A private tool for the YouTube channel Gary Finds Out.

This tool uploads short animated videos that the channel owner makes to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, on a schedule. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read basic information about that channel and its videos (titles, upload status, view and like counts).

What it stores. The login token is stored only on the owner's own computer or server. No YouTube data is stored anywhere else.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with or sent to anyone. Nothing is used for advertising.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. For questions, contact the channel owner through the support email shown on the consent screen.
